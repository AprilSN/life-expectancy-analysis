Life Expectancy Dataset Variable Description

Country - the name of the country
Continent - the continent the country lies on
Region - the region the country lies in
Infant_deaths - number of infant deaths per 1000 live births
Under_five_deaths - number of deaths of children aged <5 per 1000 live births
Adult_mortality - number of deaths aged 15-60 per 1000 population; calculated as the probability of dying between the ages 15 and 60 per year among a hypothetical cohort of 100 000 people from the given population
Alcohol_consumption -  average alcohol consumption in liters per capita
BMI - average body mass index of the country
Hepatitis_B - percentage of Hepatitis B (HepB3) immunisation coverage among 1-year-olds
Measles - percentage of Measles (MCV1) immunisation coverage among 1-year-olds
Polio - percentage of Polio (Pol3) immunisation coverage among 1-year-olds
Diphtheria - percentage of Diphtheria tetanus toxoid and pertussis (DTP3) immunisation coverage among 1-year-olds
Incidents_HIV - incidents of HIV positivity per 1000 population aged 15-49
GDP - GDP per capita in USD
Population_mln - population in millions
Thinness_ten_nineteen_years - percentage of children aged 10-19 with body mass index below 2 standard deviations below the median
Thinness_five_nine_years - percentage of children aged 5-9 with body mass index below 2 standard deviations below the median
Schooling - average number of years people aged 25+ had spent in formal education
Economy_status_Developing - dummy variable indicating whether the country registered its status as developing with WTO
Economy_status_Developed - dummy variable indicating whether the country registered its status as developed with WTO
Life_expectancy - average life expectancy of the population
